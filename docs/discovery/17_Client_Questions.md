# 17 — Client Questions

Questions StateNext Labs should ask SOC Alliance, separated by urgency. This list is informed by concrete gaps and discrepancies found during research (`02_Organization_Research.md`, `03_Current_Website_Audit.md`) — not generic boilerplate.

## Critical Before Development

**Organizational History & Facts**
1. What is the organization's actual founding story and year? The current site says "established in 1995," but the 501(c)(3) IRS ruling year is 2015 — can you clarify this gap?
2. Is SOC Alliance related to, or the successor of, an entity called "Sigma Omega Foundation" (same EIN, per public nonprofit registries)? May this be referenced on a new About/History page, and if so, how should it be described?
3. What is the current, accurate Board of Directors roster and each member's title? The website and the most recent Form 990 disagree on at least two points (a director present on the 990 but not the website; conflicting Treasurer names).
4. May we obtain real headshots and short bios for the Executive Director and each board member?

**Content & Programs**
5. What is the "Building Pledge Agreement" for specifically — what capital project, what is its cost/timeline, and why does it matter to donors?
6. What are your current, accurate program-outcome statistics (e.g., scholarships awarded per year, number of mentees served, Talent Hunt participants, jobs created via economic-development programming, health-program reach)? We found only one public figure ("$100,000+ in scholarships awarded" cumulatively via the Mardi Gras Fundraiser) and do not want to publish anything beyond what you confirm.
7. What is SOC Alliance's formal service area — is it Woodlawn specifically, Fuller Park, or citywide Chicago (the current site's messaging is inconsistent on this)?
8. Which named partners (Cook County, IDHS, Fuller Park Advisory Council, Kroc Center, Westside Justice Center, Sigma Omega Chapter, and any others) may we list and show a logo for on the new site?

**Legal, Financial & Compliance**
9. May we publish your most recent Form 990/990-EZ or an annual-report summary on a new Financials & Transparency page?
10. Who currently manages your Donorbox account, and can we get access to configure an embedded (rather than off-site-linked) donation widget?
11. Do you have (or want) a formal Privacy Policy? The current site does not appear to have one, and the redesign will collect personal data through several forms.
12. For the Monthly Pledge / Building Pledge agreements — are you open to moving these to a dedicated e-signature platform (e.g., DocuSign)? Who would manage that account?

**Technical & Access**
13. Who currently manages the domain registration and DNS for socalliance.org, and can we get (or be granted) access as part of this project?
14. Can you confirm the current CMS/hosting platform, and put us in touch with Olive + Ash / Olive Street Design for a handoff conversation?
15. Do you have existing brand guidelines, a logo file (vector/high-res), or brand color preferences we should use instead of, or alongside, the direction proposed in `10_Design_Direction.md`?
16. Who on your team will manage day-to-day content updates after launch (news posts, event listings), and what is their comfort level with a CMS interface?

## Can Be Confirmed During Development

**Content Details**
17. Do you have existing photography of programs, events, and board members we can use, or should we scope a photography session as part of this project?
18. Are there additional social media channels beyond Facebook that should be linked?
19. What specific volunteer opportunities currently exist or could be formalized for a new Volunteer page?
20. Are there sponsorship tiers/benefits beyond the Mardi Gras Fundraiser that should be presented on a year-round Partner page?

**Forms & Integrations**
21. Do you want application intake (jobs, mentoring, Talent Hunt) consolidated into the new site's own forms, or should we keep using Google Forms for any of these?
22. Do you use, or want, an email newsletter platform (Mailchimp, Constant Contact, etc.)?
23. Do you use any existing analytics tool, or should we set one up from scratch?

**Events**
24. Beyond the Mardi Gras Fundraiser, Talent Hunt Competition, and Giving Tuesday, are there other recurring annual events that should have dedicated pages?

**Future Functionality**
25. Would you be interested in a searchable calendar/events system in a future phase, or is a simpler list sufficient for now?
26. Is a donor CRM or volunteer-management system a future priority, or should the site stay content-focused for now?

**Accessibility & Legal**
27. Are there any specific accessibility needs among your current community/staff we should be especially mindful of in testing?
28. Are there any legal disclaimers specific to the violence-prevention/mentoring programs that should appear alongside that content?
