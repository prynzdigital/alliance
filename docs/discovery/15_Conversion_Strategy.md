# 15 — Conversion Strategy

For each conversion goal: **Current barrier → Recommended improvement → CTA.**

## Donations
**Current barrier:** an off-site handoff to an unbranded Donorbox link with no tax-deductibility, EIN, or security messaging nearby, plus a stale "Mardi Gras 2026" category still live months after the event (`03_Current_Website_Audit.md`, Section 4).
**Recommended improvement:** embed Donorbox on-page (see `14_Technical_Architecture.md`); state 501(c)(3)/EIN and tax-deductibility immediately beside the widget; offer a one-time/monthly toggle; tie suggested gift amounts to concrete outcomes (e.g., "$X funds one Talent Hunt scholarship application"); auto-archive time-bound campaigns.
**CTA:** "Donate Now" (persistent in header) and "Give Monthly" as a secondary framing.

## Program Participation (Scholarships, Mentoring, etc.)
**Current barrier:** thin program pages, no evergreen Talent Hunt page, and a mentoring/life-coaching page that is both hard to find (missing from the sitemap) and low on reassuring context for a vulnerable audience.
**Recommended improvement:** dedicated, evergreen pillar and program pages with clear eligibility, deadlines, and a warm, plain-language tone; fix the sitemap indexing gap; add a direct phone/text option alongside the mentoring application form.
**CTA:** "Apply Now" / "Learn if You're Eligible" / "Reach Out — We're Here to Help."

## Contact Inquiries
**Current barrier:** one generic contact form serves every type of inquiry (donor, applicant, media, volunteer) with no differentiation or map to help someone locate the org in person.
**Recommended improvement:** an intent-selector on the contact form (general / program / media / volunteer / partnership) and an embedded map — both currently absent (`03_Current_Website_Audit.md`, Section 10 #13).
**CTA:** "Send Us a Message," with the button label adapting to the selected intent where feasible (e.g., "Send Your Application Question").

## Volunteer Interest
**Current barrier:** no volunteer page or sign-up path exists anywhere on the current site — "volunteerism" is only a sub-bullet under the Community pillar.
**Recommended improvement:** a dedicated Get Involved → Volunteer page listing specific, current opportunities by pillar and time commitment, with a simple sign-up form (see `07_Recommended_Sitemap.md`).
**CTA:** "Volunteer With Us."

## Partnerships (Business & Institutional)
**Current barrier:** sponsorship information exists only in the context of the Mardi Gras Fundraiser; there is no year-round corporate/institutional partnership page, and no partner-logo trust-building anywhere despite several real, named partnerships.
**Recommended improvement:** a Get Involved → Partner With Us page presenting sponsorship tiers and general partnership options year-round, plus a partner-logo wall (pending each partner's confirmation to be listed publicly).
**CTA:** "Become a Partner" / "Sponsor Our Work."

## Event Participation
**Current barrier:** events live only as one-off blog posts that go stale, are never updated with a recap, and are hard to distinguish from past/archived content on the homepage's mixed "Updates" feed.
**Recommended improvement:** dedicated, evergreen event pages for recurring flagship events (Talent Hunt, Mardi Gras Fundraiser, Giving Tuesday) with clear "Upcoming" vs. "Past" status, and a structured, categorized News & Events hub replacing the undifferentiated blog.
**CTA:** "RSVP" / "Buy Tickets" / "Save the Date."

## Community Engagement (Newsletter, Social Follow)
**Current barrier:** no newsletter signup exists anywhere on the site, and only one social channel (Facebook) is linked, with no in-context prompts to follow it.
**Recommended improvement:** a simple, low-friction newsletter signup in the footer and homepage; social links surfaced more prominently, especially near news/event content.
**CTA:** "Stay Connected" / "Get Updates."

## Cross-Cutting Conversion Principle
Every barrier above shares a root cause identified across the audit: **the current site asks visitors to act (give, apply, sign a legal pledge, contact) without first giving them the context or trust signals a reasonable person would want before acting.** The conversion strategy for this redesign is therefore not primarily about adding more buttons — it's about giving every existing and new CTA the surrounding context (transparency, clarity, warmth, and evidence of legitimacy) that the current site consistently omits.
