# 09 — Content Strategy

## Content Audit: Keep, Rewrite, Reorganize, Retire

### Content Worth Keeping (as factual basis — copy should still be tightened)
- The four-pillar framework and its named sub-programs (`/community-involvement`) — the single strongest, most substantive content on the current site.
- Mission and vision statements — clear and usable as-is.
- Contact information (address, phone, email) — accurate and should carry forward.
- The factual record of programs, events, and partnerships documented in `02_Organization_Research.md` — this is real, verified organizational history and should anchor the new site's content, not be discarded.

### Content That Should Be Rewritten
- **All donation/pledge copy** (`/donate`, `/pledges`, `/monthly-pledge`, `/building-pledge-agreement`) — currently generic, single-sentence, and missing the trust and context elements identified in `03_Current_Website_Audit.md` (Section 4, Section 10 #9).
- **Violence Prevention page copy** — currently reads as a bare job posting; needs a real program description, written with the dignity-first framing recommended in `06_Strategy.md` and `05_Competitor_Analysis.md`, separated clearly from the SVPI careers content.
- **Board of Directors copy** — name/title lists only; needs real bios once the client supplies them.
- **Mentoring & Life Coaching page copy** — needs warmer, more explanatory language given the sensitivity of its audience (see `04_User_Audience_Analysis.md`, Journey C).

### Content That Should Be Reorganized
- The **Board of Directors block**, currently duplicated in full on at least five pages, should live only on its own Leadership page, linked from elsewhere.
- **Event-specific blog posts** (Talent Hunt recaps, Mardi Gras recaps) should be restructured as "past highlights" sections under evergreen program pages rather than standalone posts that go stale and are never revisited.
- The **Pledges** content should be merged into the Donate section as `Donate → Pledge a Gift`, with explanatory content added before the legal form.

### Content That Appears Outdated
- The "Mardi Gras 2026" donation category live on `/donate` months after the event (see `03_Current_Website_Audit.md`, Section 5).
- The homepage's "Updates From The Alliance" feed mixing 2021–2025 content with no archiving.
- Blog posts describing events in future/present tense with no post-event recap or outcome noted.

### Missing Information (content gaps with no current source page)
- An About/History page (does not exist — `/about` 404s).
- A Financials & Transparency page (does not exist).
- A Volunteer page (does not exist).
- A general (non-Mardi-Gras) sponsorship/partnership page (does not exist).
- A newsletter signup (does not exist anywhere).
- Quantified program-outcome statistics beyond the single Mardi Gras cumulative figure.
- Board member bios and real photographs.

### Pages Needing Stronger Calls to Action
- **Community Involvement** — describes multiple joinable programs with zero application/signup links.
- **Pledges** — asks for a legal financial commitment with no explanatory "why" first.
- **Violence Prevention** — the application CTA exists but with no program context to build the confidence needed to act on it.

## Content Rules for This Redesign

1. **No fabricated facts, statistics, testimonials, dates, or program details.** Every number or quote in the launched site must trace to either the current website, a client-confirmed source, or newly client-supplied material.
2. **Every date-bound item (events, donation campaigns) must have a clear archiving mechanism** so the "stale content" problem identified in the audit cannot recur structurally.
3. **Sensitive content (violence prevention, mentoring for people affected by firearm violence) must be written with dignity-first, trauma-informed language**, reviewed by the client before publishing.
4. **Any content referencing the organization's founding history or its possible relationship to "Sigma Omega Foundation" must be explicitly approved by the client** — this is currently an inference from external nonprofit-registry data, not a confirmed fact (see `02_Organization_Research.md`, Section 1).

A complete, itemized list of information the client must supply before final copy can be written is in `20_Client_Input_Required.md`.
