The 2025 edition was held March 22, 2025 at Vandercook College of Music; the winner was Tiffany Tyus (piano); co-sponsored by the Sigma Omega Chapter [VERIFIED-WEBSITE].
- **Omega Mardi Gras Scholarship Fundraiser** — SOC Alliance's flagship annual gala, co-hosted with the Sigma Omega Chapter of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. The 2026 edition ("21st Annual") is scheduled for March 14, 2026 at Rate Field, with sponsorships starting at $500 and a stated cumulative impact of "over $100,000 in scholarships awarded" throughout the event's history [VERIFIED-WEBSITE / VERIFIED-EXTERNAL — Ticket Falcon listing]. The "20th"/"21st annual" framing dates the event's origin to roughly 2005 **[ASSUMPTION/INFERENCE]**.
- **Fuller Park Advisory Council (FPAC) Partnership** — an August 2025 kickoff meeting joining SVPI, the Chicago Park District's Fuller Park Fieldhouse, and FPAC. Identified community priorities included traffic safety, the need for male mentors, creative youth opportunities, and transportation access as a barrier to event attendance [VERIFIED-WEBSITE].
- **Cook County Starting Block Grant (2024)** — a capacity-building grant for community-based organizations with budgets under $1 million; SOC Alliance's specific award amount was not published in any source found [VERIFIED-WEBSITE / VERIFIED-EXTERNAL]. Stated intended use: expanding youth educational services, health outreach, community support networks, and support for local entrepreneurs.
- **Blood Drive at Kroc Center** (2023) and an **"Interacting with Law Enforcement" workshop** (Oct 2023, presented by April Preyer, Esq., with the Westside Justice Center) — both one-off community events [VERIFIED-WEBSITE].
- **Giving Tuesday** — SOC Alliance's recurring year-end campaign; the 2025 edition carried a stated $10,000 goal framed around all four pillars [VERIFIED-WEBSITE].

## 10. Financial & Nonprofit-Status Data

- **Most recent financials (FY ending Oct 2024):** Revenue $82,682 · Expenses $64,999 · Net income $17,683 · Total assets $175,938 · Total liabilities $25,782 · Net assets $150,156.
- **Revenue mix:** Contributions 64.2% · Program services 24.2% · Investment income 0.2% · Other 11.4%.
- **All officers reported $0 compensation** in the most recent filing — consistent with an all-volunteer leadership structure.
- **Charity Navigator:** not yet rated (does not meet the minimum three-plus years of e-filed 990s required for a star rating).
- Asset growth from $12,898 (FY2015) to $175,938 (FY2024) — roughly 13x growth over the organization's first decade of federal filings, useful context that this is a genuinely small but growing organization.

**Scoping implication:** this financial profile (sub-$100K annual revenue, all-volunteer officers) should directly inform the technical architecture recommendation in `14_Technical_Architecture.md` — the redesign should favor low ongoing cost and low maintenance burden over enterprise-grade complexity.
